## 7. Student Learning Time (SLT) / Effective Learning Time (ELT)

Jadual mingguan (Week 1–15) memetakan CLO, Learning & Teaching Activities (F2F Physical/Online, Non-F2F Independent Learning) dan TOTAL SLT bagi setiap minggu. Termasuk sub-jumlah untuk Continuous Assessment dan Final Assessment, serta pengiraan:

- A: % SLT for F2F Physical Component
- B: % SLT for Online & Independent Learning Component
- C: % SLT for Online Component (30–79% = PTG / Pembelajaran Teradun Gantian)
- D, D1, D2: % SLT for Practical Components

> Demonstrate integrity while working effectively with others to accomplish a work-based project.

**CLO2 asal:**
> Classify proficiently work-based projects, including effective planning and control.

**CLO2 selepas ditambah ESD: Strategic Thinking (STT):**
> Classify proficiently work-based projects, including effective and strategic planning and control.
